Frequently Asked Questions

Who owns the website?

You own the website! While you're using our service, we handle hosting, maintenance, and updates. If you decide to move on, you can take your website with you or we can help transfer it to another hosting provider.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your service at any time with no cancellation fees. We believe in providing flexibility for our clients.

What if I need updates?

All our plans include regular updates. For the Starter plan, we handle security updates and minor content changes. The Pro plan includes more extensive updates and content creation.

How does the AI content customization work?

Our AI analyzes your business type, target audience, and industry trends to generate optimized content including text, images, and SEO elements. You can review and customize the AI-generated content before publishing.

Do you offer e-commerce functionality?

Yes, our Pro plan includes e-commerce functionality with product listings, shopping cart, and secure payment processing integration.
